I build small tools for people who make software.

For fifteen years I've worked in the gap between design and engineering — mostly in finance, government and retail. The pattern repeats: a team is slowed by something nobody owns, so I build the missing piece.

Right now that's Plugma, a command-line tool for building Figma plugins. Before that a browser IDE, a prompt library, and a handful of plugins used by designers every week.

Everything I make is small, composable, and documented. Most of it is on GitHub.

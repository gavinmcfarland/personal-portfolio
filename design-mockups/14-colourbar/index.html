Design engineer. Builds the tools design systems are made with, and the systems themselves.

01 Name

gavin — design engineer; builds the tools design systems are made with, and the systems themselves.

02 Synopsis

$ gavin [--design-systems] [--front-end] [--tooling]

$ gavin --hire [--remote | --london]

03 Description

Fifteen years between design and front-end, mostly spent on the seam: token pipelines, component APIs, and the editor plugins that keep the two halves honest with each other.

Every tool listed under work(1) began as a problem that kept recurring, and exists because the thing that would have fixed it did not.

specimen(7) · design system reference

The sixteen-colour control strip, the two system faces, and the five-step flat type ramp — printed as itself, not described.

01 Name

14 · colourbar — a cross of the man(7) skeleton (mono structure, numbered sections, a $ prompt) with the press control strip: flat cells of the Windows 95 sixteen, divided by hard 1px rules, ink near-black on white stock. No curves, no shadows, no gradients, no webfont.

02 Colour tokens · light + dark

06 Strips, marks & grid the illustration engine

No dot, hatch, screen, stipple or gradient anywhere below — every cell is one solid background-color from the sixteen. This is the whole illustration system: six strip geometries, three marks, and a 12-column construction grid.

Construction grid — press G, or the grid button in the rail, to lay the live 12-column overlay over this sheet.

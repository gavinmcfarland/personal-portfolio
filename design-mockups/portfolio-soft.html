About

Two disciplines, one job

I started in graphic design and ended up writing the code too — mostly out of impatience.

Handing a beautiful static file to someone else and hoping felt like the wrong end of the job. These days I sit in the gap between the two: close enough to design to argue about optical spacing, close enough to engineering to know what that argument costs in render time.

What I actually do

Mostly design systems and the tooling around them — component libraries, token pipelines, documentation nobody wanted to write, and the occasional plugin that saves a team an afternoon a week.

The boring parts

How a thing behaves at 320px. What happens on a slow connection. Whether the focus ring is visible. Whether the build is still fast in a year. That’s where the quality lives.

Off the clock

Too many half-finished side projects, a long-running interest in typography, and a habit of rewriting things that already worked fine.

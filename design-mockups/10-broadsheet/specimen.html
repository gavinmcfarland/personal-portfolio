The Working Sheet — a broadsheet run for one voice across twelve structures.

Newsprint stock, a serif that argues and a condensed sans that organises, two spot inks printed flat the way a two-colour press would — under a doctrine of no curves, no shadows, no gradients, and flat type ranked by rule, inversion and number rather than by size.

0. Specimen

Windows 95, taken at its word.

System chrome, VGA colour and MS Sans Serif — flat bevels standing in for depth, square corners, nothing blurred and nothing floating.

This is the specimen sheet for the Workstation mockup — every colour token, type size and control below is drawn live from this mockup's own system.css, so it can be flipped against the other portfolio directions and compared like for like.

Open View → High contrast in the menu above to see the whole sheet re-theme — that toggle is the real one, backed by the same localStorage['gm-theme'] key the rest of the site uses.

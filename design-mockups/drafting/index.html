General notes

Read in conjunction with Sheet A

01 I started in graphic design and ended up writing the code too, mostly out of impatience. Handing over a beautiful static file and hoping for the best always felt like the wrong end of the job.

02 These days I sit in the gap: close enough to design to argue about optical spacing, close enough to engineering to know what that argument costs in render time. Most of the work is design systems and the tooling around them.

03 I care about the parts nobody demos — how a component behaves at 320px, what happens on a slow connection, whether the focus ring is visible, whether the build is still fast in a year's time.

04 Outside of that: too many half-finished side projects, a long-running interest in typography, and a habit of rewriting things that already worked fine.

About

I started in graphic design and ended up writing the code too — mostly out of impatience. Handing a beautiful static file to someone else and hoping felt like the wrong end of the job.

These days I sit in the gap between the two: close enough to design to argue about optical spacing, close enough to engineering to know what that argument costs in render time.

Most of the work is design systems and the tooling around them — the unglamorous plumbing that decides whether a team ships consistently or just talks about it.

That means component libraries, token pipelines, documentation nobody wanted to write, and the occasional plugin that saves a team an afternoon a week.

I care about the boring parts: how a thing behaves at 320px, what happens on a slow connection, whether the focus ring is visible, whether the build is still fast in a year.

Outside of that — too many half-finished side projects, a long-running interest in typography, and a habit of rewriting things that already worked fine.

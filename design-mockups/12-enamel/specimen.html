Why a graphite ground

Vitreous enamel is fused onto steel, not paper — the ground is worked, mid-dark, and never pretends to be a printed sheet.

Why only three sizes

Rank is made by fill, inversion, weight, rule, numbering, edge marker and space instead — eight devices, judged side by side in signal.html.

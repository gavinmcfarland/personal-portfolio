Most teams are slowed by something nobody owns.

I build that thing, then write down how it works.

Fifteen years between design and engineering — finance, government and retail. The pattern repeats: something falls between two teams, nobody is given it, and the work slows down until somebody builds the missing piece.

Right now that is plugma, a toolchain for building Figma plugins. Its changelog and the icon set used across this sheet are both here.

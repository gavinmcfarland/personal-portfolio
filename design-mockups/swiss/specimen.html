Specimen

The design system behind the Swiss Editorial mockup — a Vignelli / Pentagram lineage of strict 12-column grids, hairline rules and a display masthead.

Tinos serif for prose, Inter sans for structure, and a single CV-purple accent. Everything below is rendered in the mockup's own tokens, so it themes light and dark exactly as the portfolio does.

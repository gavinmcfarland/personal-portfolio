sheet 05 / 08 · specimen

The system, laid flat. Every colour, size, texture and treatment the nine sheets are allowed to use — and nothing else. If it is not on this page, it is not in the system.

01

palette

Muted stock does the talking. The five bold flats appear only as filled blocks or bordered chips, each with its own paired ink. No gradients — nothing here blends.

body · 15

Archivo carries argument. This is the only prose size in the system; rank comes from numbering, inversion, rules and pattern — not from scale.

Five hard-stop patterns. One texture per surface, drawn in the surface's own ink. A gradient that does not grade is a pattern.

Where an icon would go, a transparent square with a border goes instead. The system never pretends to have drawn an icon it has not. 16 · 24 · 40, hatched when a slot is reserved. Used sparingly — never inside tables.

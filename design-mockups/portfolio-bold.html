Two disciplines, one head.

I started in graphic design and ended up writing the code too — mostly out of impatience. Handing a beautiful static file to someone else and hoping felt like the wrong end of the job.

These days I sit in the gap: close enough to design to argue about optical spacing, close enough to engineering to know what that argument costs in render time. Most of my work is design systems and the tooling around them — the unglamorous plumbing that decides whether a team ships consistently or just talks about it.

Outside of that: too many half-finished side projects, a long-running interest in typography, and a habit of rewriting things that already worked fine.

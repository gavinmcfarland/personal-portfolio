Selected work

2021 — 2024

The gap

Most of the interesting problems live between the disciplines.

A beautiful spec meets the reality of shipping it, and something gets lost. I've spent fifteen years in that gap. The pattern repeats: a team is slowed by something nobody owns, so I build the missing piece.

01

Design systems

Tokens, components, and the governance that keeps them honest — so the design file and the codebase describe the same thing, and stay that way after I leave.

02

Product & front-end

Interface design carried through to production code. I still start every project in a design file, because a problem you can't draw is one you don't understand yet.

03

Developer tooling

CLIs, plugins and build steps — the small piece of infrastructure nobody owns. Usually the difference between a day of setup and a single command.

Process

Short loops and visible artefacts. You should always be able to see what I'm working on, and the diagnosis arrives in writing before the scope does.

Handover

Production code in your repo, in your conventions, reviewed by your team as it lands. Documentation and a walkthrough at the end. The measure of the work is whether it outlives the engagement.

Prose

A Figma plugin is two programs that can't see each other. One runs in a sandbox with access to the document; the other runs in an iframe with access to the DOM.

Change one line and you rebuild and reload by hand. I had assembled that same toolchain enough times to stop pretending it was a one-off.

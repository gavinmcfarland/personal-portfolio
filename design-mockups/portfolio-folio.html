summary

I build the small tools teams are missing.

Fifteen years in the gap between design and engineering — finance, government and retail. The pattern repeats: a team is slowed by something nobody owns, so I build the missing piece and document it before I go.

Right now that is Plugma, a toolchain for building Figma plugins. Before it, a browser IDE, a prompt library, and a handful of plugins designers use every week. Most of it is on GitHub.
